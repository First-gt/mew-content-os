3. Camera, Lighting & Sound Specifications

Camera & Lighting Setup

  • Main Camera (A-Cam): Eye-level Medium Close-Up, 35mm / 50mm f/2.0
  • Secondary Camera (B-Cam): 45-degree Profile Close-Up or Overhead Desk Shot
  • Key Light: Softbox 45-degree angle @ 5600K Daylight, subtle fill
  • Background & Props: Clean warm wood desk, notebook, muted canvas tones (#fbfaf8 / #f4f2ee)

Sound Design & Voice Recording

  • Microphone: Lav mic or boom condenser placed within 20cm, zero echo
  • Voice Delivery: Measured, unhurried cadence (~130 words per minute)
  • SFX Elements: Subtle tactile clock ticks, smooth whooshes on cuts, clean page flip
  • Background Music: Ambient low-tempo instrumental ducked to -24dB under speech
